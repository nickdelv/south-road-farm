Our Story

It started with a wooden arch

2022

The wedding that
started everything.

Hi, we're Nick and Dave.

We're brothers-in-law and co-owners of South Road Farm in Fayette, Maine.

In Fall 2022, Nick got married at a barn in New England. His wife, Alex, was in the middle of her medical fellowship, so Nick ended up planning most of it, and he loved it.

This was his one shot at the wedding he'd always wanted to be invited to. All the people they love were there, and the only assigned seats were the couple's own. Food trucks over fine dining. Fire pits over anything formal. Lots of small spaces to spread out, indoors and out. Pies over cake and sneakers over stilettos.

More of a festival than a function.

They said their vows under a wooden arch Dave built for them as a gift. Nobody had asked him to. He just made it.

"This is one of the best weddings I've ever been to. Maybe the best."

— A friend of Nick's dad, at the end of the night

That's when Nick realized he loved this, and he wanted to build a space that could host the same kind of unique, laid-back, one-of-a-kind weekend for other couples.

Dave, a carpenter who has spent decades renovating old buildings, had a similar long time dream of creating a venue like this. So they went looking, and in 2023 they found South Road Farm: two barns, a farmhouse, and views that open up for miles behind the buildings.

That arch now stands at South Road Farm, and couples who book here can say their own vows under it. We'd love to host yours.

What Makes Us Different

Not a rental. A weekend.

You get the whole farm, all weekend.

From midday Friday to midday Sunday, both barns, the grounds and the farmhouse are yours. We only do one wedding per weekend, so nobody else's guests are around and nobody else's schedule is running.

You sleep here.

The farmhouse sleeps 10 and comes with the weekend. Your closest people are steps from the party, and you all wake up to breakfast together.

Your people, your plan.

Bring your own food trucks or caterer, your own bar and your own music. There's no package to pick from and no one telling you what the night has to look like. It gets to be 100% you. We'll point you toward vendors who already know the property.

Room to spread out.

Two barns, decks, and a lot of land to wander. Guests move between small spaces, indoors and out, instead of staying in one room all night.

Old charm, wedding-ready.

The barns and farmhouse are more than 200 years old, and the hand-hewn beams and wide pine floors are still the first thing you notice. We left those alone. Then we made the place work for a real wedding weekend, with bathrooms, heat, power, lighting, an accessible entrance, and a rain plan.

The Land

200+ years of history

This farm has been here since 1793. We've become obsessed with the history of who lived here, what the buildings used to be, and how it all held together for over two centuries. The full story — from Joseph Watson's original settlement through the restoration Dave did largely by hand — is worth the read.
