The Experience

More of a festival
than a function

Your whole family and your closest friends, together for a whole weekend. Friday night welcome drinks. The ceremony outside, with the view behind you, then food trucks, signature drinks, your favorite music, fire pits and a dance floor in the barn. Nobody's stuck in one seat in one room all night — everyone's up, moving around, wandering out to see the view.

Sunday morning is coffee in the farmhouse kitchen and last night's stories, with nobody rushing out.

It's the best party you could throw at home, just bigger, with an incredible view.

Our Story

It started with
a wedding arch

In 2022, Nick's brother-in-law Dave — a lifelong carpenter — built a wooden arch for Nick's own wedding. That arch still stands on the ceremony lawn. Dave has spent the years since restoring nearly everything you see here: both barns, the farmhouse, every deck. By hand, largely himself. This place has the feeling it has because of how it was made.
