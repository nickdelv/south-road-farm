Pricing

$10,000

Full weekend, introductory rate. $15,000 regular price — this is our rate for weddings booked before April 2027, for 2027 and 2028 dates. Midday Friday to midday Sunday, with the farmhouse included.

What's included.

Your weekend includes

  • Both barns and the decks
  • The grounds, with fire pits and Adirondack chairs
  • Outdoor lighting
  • Tables and chairs
  • Hand-built ceremony benches and the wooden arch Dave made
  • The farmhouse, fully furnished, sleeps 10
  • Parking for approximately 50 vehicles
  • The whole property from midday Friday to midday Sunday, with no other wedding on site

You bring

  • Food, whether trucks or a caterer
  • Drinks and a licensed bartender
  • Music
  • Decor and any additional rentals (linens, cocktail tables, etc.)
  • A day-of coordinator

We'll connect you with vendors we trust who already know the property.

Add-on

Thursday arrival.

Thursday arrival: +$2,000. Want an extra day to set up or settle in? Add Thursday to your weekend.

A few ground rules.

  • A professional wedding planner or day-of coordinator
  • A licensed caterer
  • Licensed bar service
  • A shuttle service if you expect more than 50 vehicles
  • Outdoor amplified music ends at 10 PM, indoor at 11 PM

What it adds up to.

South Road Farm is bring-your-own-vendor, so here's what everything else typically costs. Ranges are based on 2026 quotes from Maine and New England vendors and assume a full weekend at the farm.

Venue

  • Full weekend (Friday–Sunday) $15,000
  • Founding pricing for our first 2027 couples $10,000

Includes both barns, the farmhouse, tables and chairs, ceremony benches, the arch, and restrooms.

Vendor ranges

  • Catering $75–$115 per guest
  • Bar service (bartenders, setup, ice, supplies) $1,500–$3,000
  • Alcohol (BYOB, store-bought) $10–$20 per guest
  • Additional rentals (linens, cocktail tables, etc.) $5–$15 per guest
  • Florals $2,500–$5,500
  • Photography (6–8 hours) $3,500–$7,500
  • DJ (ceremony plus 5–6 hours) $1,800–$2,500
  • Wedding planner (required) $2,500–$4,000
  • Event liability insurance (required) $150–$300

Florals: personal flowers, ceremony arch and centerpieces. Bigger installs, like greenery on the beams, add more.

A typical 100-guest weekend runs about $20,000–$38,000 in vendors, plus the venue.

Updated for 2027. Every wedding is different, and we're happy to walk you through the numbers on a call .
